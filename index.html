Rules & Privacy

Important information about using MindBridge.

🔐 Privacy

Personal information should only be shared with authorized individuals according to the client's preferences and applicable privacy requirements.

Therapists are expected to follow applicable professional standards, laws, regulations, and organizational policies.

MindBridge should not be treated as a replacement for emergency services.

🛡️ Security

Production implementation must include appropriate authentication, encryption, access controls, audit logging, and secure data storage.
